Venue

LOCO 2026 will take place in Margrethe Bohr Salen at the Niels Bohr Building, University of Copenhagen.

  • Institution: University of Copenhagen
  • Building: Niels Bohr Building
  • Room: Auditorium 001-0-EF.000 – Margrethe Bohr Salen

Travel information

  • By metro: Skjolds Plads on the M3 line is the closest metro station. From the station, continue on foot toward Jagtvej and the Niels Bohr Building.
  • From Copenhagen Airport: The M2 metro departs from Terminal 3. Travel to Kongens Nytorv, change to the M3 line, and continue to Skjolds Plads.
  • From Copenhagen Central Station: Board the M3 line at København H and travel directly to Skjolds Plads.
  • By taxi: Ask the driver to take you to the Niels Bohr Building, Jagtvej 155, 2200 København N.
  • Journey planning: Check current routes, departure times, and possible service changes using Rejseplanen .
