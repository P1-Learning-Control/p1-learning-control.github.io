About the workshop

The first workshop of the P1 program Learning and Optimal Control in Dynamical Systems will bring together researchers working at the intersection of learning, optimization, reinforcement learning, control, and dynamical systems.

The goal of the workshop is to create a focused setting for scientific exchange, open discussion, and collaboration across institutions and research communities.

Details

  • Date: 10th September 2026
  • Time: 9:00-17:00
  • Location: Copenhagen, Denmark
  • Format: In person
  • Registration: The Registration is not open yet.

Topics

  • Learning-based control and decision-making
  • Optimal control and optimization for dynamical systems
  • Reinforcement learning and adaptive control
  • Safety, robustness, and guarantees in learning-enabled systems
  • Applications in robotics, energy systems, transportation, and related domains
